Privacy

This toolkit is built to keep work on your device.

What stays local

  • Text, passwords, tokens, and hashes
  • Images drawn onto a canvas
  • JSON, CSV, HTML, and generated tags
  • Printable invoices and documents

What may leave the browser

Only when you press Resolve on the DNS tool. That request goes to Cloudflare DNS-over-HTTPS. This site does not proxy, store, or log the query.

Cookies and analytics

These pages do not set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting logs on CoreyMcDaniel.com, if any, are controlled by the parent site’s server configuration and are not used by these tools.
